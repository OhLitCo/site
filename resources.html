For people who help adults learn

Curricula, kits, and training for tutors, teachers, and program staff.

Pathway to Literacy

Immigrant Welcome Center, Indianapolis

Curriculum

A free, well-rounded curriculum and assessment tool for adult learners with emerging literacy. Includes a 10-week sequence organized into four levels (A to D), a reading diagnostic, and an intake assessment.

What we love about it

With everything hosted in Google Slides, this resource is easy to access and use.

pathwaytoliteracy.org

ESL Instructional Support Kits

Literacy Minnesota

Curriculum

A set of five free, downloadable kits: Pronunciation, Phonics and Phonemic Awareness, Speaking and Listening, Reading, and Citizenship. Each is packed with grab-and-go activities for one-on-one, small group, or full class settings.

What we love about it

Grab-and-go activities that actually work. Low prep, high utility.

literacymn.org

CILIA-T

University of Minnesota Duluth / Literacy Minnesota

Curriculum

A comprehensive, research-backed curriculum for intermediate and advanced adult English language learners, focused on academic vocabulary, comprehension, and digital literacy skills.

What we love about it

It pushes learners toward the academic language needed for higher education, career advancement, and citizenship. Rigorous and free.

CILIA-T site

Phonemic Awareness in Older Learners: Bridging the Gap

edWeb.net Teach to Read Community

Video / PD

A free on-demand webinar exploring how phonemic awareness instruction can be a game-changer for older students and adult learners who struggle with word reading. Educators can earn a free CE certificate upon completion.

What we love about it

Phonemic awareness doesn't stop being relevant after second grade, and this webinar makes that case well.

Watch on edWeb

LINCS

U.S. Department of Education

Website

A national site for adult education practitioners with free self-paced online courses and a searchable resource collection. It also has a Learner Center that connects adults to free online resources for reading and job skills.

lincs.ed.gov

Free Webinars for Tutors and Teachers

ProLiteracy

Video / PD

Recent topics include the updated citizenship test, teaching foundational reading skills to adult ESOL learners, health literacy, and moving from ESL to employment. Some resources on the site need a free account.

proliteracy.org/courses

Money Smart for Adults

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC)

Curriculum

An instructor-led financial education curriculum with 14 modules. Each one comes with an instructor guide, slides, and a participant guide you can download. Other languages, including Spanish, are available through the FDIC catalog.

fdic.gov

Free Reading Resources for Beginning-Level, US-Born Adult Learners

Literacy Minnesota

Website

A short roundup of free reading practice for adults who grew up speaking English and are building basic reading skills. It points to Reading Skills for Today's Adults, ReadWorks, and Newsela Lite. ReadWorks and Newsela Lite need a free login, and ReadWorks was made for kids.

literacymn.org

Running a program

Help for starting, strengthening, and sustaining a program, whether you are paid staff or a volunteer team.

Ohio Literacy Resource Center

Kent State University

WebsiteOhio

Ohio's home for adult and family literacy support. It offers training, program management resources, standards-based lesson resources, and a Family Literacy Resource Notebook, and it works with literacy coalitions, libraries, and providers across the state.

Questions?

Toll free in Ohio: 800-765-2897, or email olrc@literacy.kent.edu.

literacy.kent.edu

Adult Learner Persistence Project

New England Literacy Resource Center

Website

Findings from 18 adult education programs that tested ways to keep learners coming back. It includes strategies for intake, instruction, support, and program design, self-assessment tools, and six "drivers of persistence."

nelrc.org/persist

The Literacy Cooperative

Greater Cleveland

WebsiteOhio

A Cleveland nonprofit working to advance literacy through a service delivery system that meets the highest standards in the field. Its vision is that all children and adults in Greater Cleveland reach their highest literacy potential.

literacycooperative.org

Funding

Grants and free tools for programs. Eligibility and dates change, so check each one before you plan around it.

Literacy Opportunity Fund

ProLiteracy

Funding

Grants for U.S. nonprofits that teach adults directly. Funds can cover general operating costs like salaries, technology, and supplies, or a specific project or new program.

Good to know

You need to be a registered 501(c)(3) with an EIN. Book distributions and lending libraries are not funded. The 2026 round closed October 1, so watch for the next one.

proliteracy.org

Mobile Learning Fund

ProLiteracy and New Readers Press

Funding

Free licenses to online learning tools for adult literacy programs, including New Readers Press Online Learning, Learning Upgrade, EnGen, Leamos, and News for You Online.

Good to know

Applications are open year round and awarded on a rolling basis while funds last. Learners must be 16 or older. You will need to complete a free demo or trial, and it does not cover devices.

proliteracy.org

Adult and Family Literacy Grants

Dollar General Literacy Foundation

Funding

Grants of up to $10,000 for adult and family literacy. Nonprofits, public libraries, and schools or colleges that provide direct literacy services can apply.

Good to know

Your organization must be within 15 miles of a Dollar General store. The 2026 adult and family deadline was in early February, so expect an early-year window.

dgliteracy.org

Innovation Grants

National Literacy Directory

Funding

Grants for programs that find successful and innovative ways to reach more learners. Any program listed in the National Literacy Directory can apply.

Good to know

The page does not post award amounts or dates, so check with the directory. Getting your program listed first is the way in.

nld.org

For adults who are learning

Free tools you can use on your own, at your own pace, on your phone or a computer.

DigitalLearn

Public Library Association

Website

A free online hub for digital literacy training. Short, video-based courses with narration on using a computer, navigating websites, email, and internet privacy. Available in English and Spanish.

What we love about it

Clean, accessible, and easy to navigate. The self-paced format works well for learners.

digitallearn.org

Learning Chocolate

learningchocolate.com

Website

A free vocabulary learning platform organized into themed categories like body, community, food, jobs, and transportation, with picture dictionaries and audio pronunciation. Learners can choose from multiple language interfaces.

What we love about it

Easy to use, visually clear, and fun. There's no login required. Learners can just jump in.

learningchocolate.com

Life Skills for Adult Learners

Rise / Articulate

Curriculum

A free interactive online course covering practical life skills, including workplace readiness, communication, financial literacy, and navigating everyday situations. Self-paced and mobile-friendly.

What we love about it

The mobile-friendly format means learners can use it on their phones, a real advantage for busy adults juggling work and family.

Open course

USA Learns

Sacramento County Office of Education

Website

A free website where adults can learn English, build job skills, and prepare for U.S. citizenship. Lessons cover speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar, pronunciation, and life skills, and they work on a phone, computer, or tablet.

usalearns.org

Bow Valley College

Ohio's Diploma Options for Adults

Ohio Department of Education and Workforce

Website

One page that lays out the ways Ohio adults can earn a high school diploma or equivalence: the Adult Workforce Diploma Program, High School Equivalence (formerly GED), the High School Continuation Program, and Aspire.

Good to know

The Adult Workforce Diploma Program opened July 1, 2026 and is no cost to adults. The 22+ Adult High School Diploma Program and the Adult Diploma Program no longer take new students.

education.ohio.gov

Khan Academy

Khan Academy

Website

Free videos, practice exercises, and progress tracking that learners can use at their own pace on a phone, tablet, or computer. It covers all four High School Equivalency areas: math, language arts, science, and social studies.

Good to know

Everything is free except Khanmigo, the AI tutor, which needs a paid subscription for learners.

khanacademy.org

VOA Learning English

Voice of America

WebsiteVideo

News stories read about one-third slower than normal speed, with captions, audio, and text. It also has lesson series for beginners (Let's Learn English) and for advanced learners (American Stories, Everyday Grammar).

learningenglish.voanews.com
