Advocacy

Adult literacy work runs on caring people and public support. Advocacy is how we make sure the people who make decisions in Ohio hear from the people who do the work.

Why it matters

Decisions about funding and policy shape what adult learners can reach.

Whether a program can keep its doors open, add a class, or hire a tutor coordinator often depends on choices made in Columbus. Legislators hear from many causes. They hear most from the people who tell them what is happening back home, and that includes you.

How the Statehouse works

The Ohio General Assembly has two chambers, the House and the Senate. Ideas become bills, bills go to committees, and committees hold hearings where people can share what a proposal would mean in real life. Anyone can follow along, and the Ohio Legislature website is the place to find schedules, committees, and your own legislators.

Ways to speak up

You do not need to be an expert. A true story is enough.

  1. 1

    Know who represents you

    Every Ohioan has one state representative and one state senator. Enter your address at the Ohio House site to find your representative and the Ohio Senate site to find your senator.

  2. 2

    Tell a short story

    A few sentences about one learner or one class can say more than a spreadsheet. Share only what people are comfortable having shared, and leave names out unless you have permission.

  3. 3

    Invite a visit

    Legislators learn the most by seeing a program in action. Invite yours to sit in on a class or a tutoring session.

  4. 4

    Show up together

    Hearings are open to the public. Many voices saying the same thing, from different corners of the state, get noticed.

How we approach it

OhLitCo shares information and ways to take part. We do not endorse candidates or parties, and we do not tell anyone how to vote. People in our network speak for themselves and for their own programs.

What is coming

We are working on plain-language guides and a simple calendar of what is happening at the Statehouse. Want to help shape it, or have a story or a question? We would like to hear it.
