Our work

1

Connection

We connect the people doing literacy work across Ohio because when we grow together, our learners grow too.

2

Resource Sharing

We make it easier to find, use, and share the tools, materials, and knowledge that already exists in our communities.

3

Support

We show up for the people doing the work with encouragement, with expertise, and with a network that has their back.

4

Advocacy

We lift our collective voice so that literacy gets the attention, funding, and policy support it deserves in Ohio.
