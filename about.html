OhLitCo

About

OhLitCo is the Ohio Literacy Coalition, a statewide coalition for the people helping Ohio adults build their reading and writing.

Our vision

A literate Ohio.

Literacy colors your world.

A life with limited literacy is like a life in grayscale. Literacy opens up the world around us in vibrant, living color. Join us as we build a colorful, literate Ohio, one word at a time.

Our mission

To equip adult learners and those helping them to find and use what they need to meet their literacy goals.

We do that through four pillars.

  1. 1

    Connection

    We connect the people doing literacy work across Ohio because when we grow together, our learners grow too. Quarterly Connect meetings are coming soon.

  2. 2

    Resource Sharing

    We make it easier to find, use, and share the tools, materials, and knowledge that already exist in our communities. Browse the resources.

  3. 3

    Support

    We show up for the people doing the work with encouragement, with expertise, and with a network that has their back.

  4. 4

    Advocacy

    We lift our collective voice so that literacy gets the attention, funding, and policy support it deserves in Ohio.

Who belongs here

Funded or volunteer, big program or small, if you are helping adults build their reading and writing, you belong here.

That includes tutors, teachers, program coordinators, funders, and adults working on their own reading and writing. Our work is about helping the helpers, and it is not about making money.

Where we are

OhLitCo is new. Right now that means a curated list of resources, plans for a quarterly Connect meeting, and a growing network of people who care about adult literacy in Ohio. We hope to grow into a coalition the whole state can lean on.
